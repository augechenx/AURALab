# Teaching

## Current Courses at RIT

### CVET-464-01: Construction Planning, Scheduling and Control
**Fall 2026** | 3 Credit Hours

This course covers planning, organization, scheduling, and control of construction projects. Students gain practice in using the Critical Path Method (CPM) in scheduling, Monte Carlo risk analysis, and monitoring the progress of construction projects. Cost control and resource allocation are also explored.
